Hold left or right of the screen to steer (Simple), or drag (Drag and Drift steering) Tap the Boost button for a burst of boost, or hold it to keep going II button to pause

Slides: with Drift steering, turn hard at speed and the car slides (Simple and Drag steering never slide); the longer the slide, the bigger the boost when you straighten up. Tank: one bar for fuel and damage. It drains as you drive and when you bump things; smashing refills it, and so do food trucks. Crazy Dash: boost from a standstill. Boost for double points and a fuller refill; while boosting at speed you Power Ram through amber objects for a little tank. Hunt your size: things far smaller than you barely score. Orange ramps launch you for air points.
